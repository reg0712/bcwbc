Classify a bank customer's complaint and route it to the team that handles it.

Categories:
- failed_transfer: a UPI, IMPS, NEFT or RTGS transfer or payment was debited but has not
  reached the beneficiary
- card_dispute: a debit or credit card charge the customer does not recognise, a duplicate card
  charge, or any other disputed card payment
- atm_cash: an ATM did not dispense cash, or dispensed less than requested, but the account was
  debited
- loan_charges: a loan EMI debit, a bounce or penal charge, or a foreclosure or prepayment charge
- account_access: the customer cannot log in to net banking or mobile banking, or the account is
  locked or frozen, including for KYC
- other: anything else

Teams: digital_payments for failed_transfer, card_disputes for card_dispute, atm_operations for
atm_cash, loan_servicing for loan_charges, account_services for account_access and customer_care
for other. If the customer reports a transaction they did not make, a lost or stolen card, or
fraud, the team is fraud_response whatever the category.

Priority is high for suspected fraud or when INR 50,000 or more is at stake, and normal otherwise.

Return JSON with three fields: category, team and priority.
